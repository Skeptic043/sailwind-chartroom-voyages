Worlds and routes are published from Sailwind Chartroom and Voyage Recorder. Published routes and race entries are public files tied to the publisher's GitHub username. Times are self-reported for fun, and the checks are designed to catch mistakes rather than cheating.

Chartroom Voyages is fan-made and not affiliated with the developers of Sailwind. Its code is free to use under the MIT License. Island outlines come from the Sailwind Web Map (MIT), and the paper texture is old paper by Flixberry Entertainment (CC0).
