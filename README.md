# Chartroom Voyages

Publish your Sailwind worlds and routes and explore their heatmaps, or race other players on community created races, all from [Sailwind Chartroom](https://github.com/Skeptic043/sailwind-chartroom/releases/latest) and [Voyage Recorder](https://github.com/Skeptic043/sailwind-voyage-recorder/releases/latest).

[Open Chartroom Voyages](https://skeptic043.github.io/sailwind-chartroom-voyages/)

## Getting started

You can browse the site without installing anything. To publish routes and race runs, you need a GitHub account, [Sailwind Chartroom](https://github.com/Skeptic043/sailwind-chartroom/releases/latest), and [Voyage Recorder](https://github.com/Skeptic043/sailwind-voyage-recorder/releases/latest) recording while you sail. Every upload is posted as a GitHub issue.

## How it works

- **Sharing a voyage:** Choose `Voyage` > `Share voyage` in Chartroom, select the part of your track you want to share, then choose `Save and open`. Drag the saved `.voyage.json` file into the form that opens and submit it. For older recordings that do not record their world, you can choose the world under `World (for older recordings)` on the form. The voyage appears on its world's page and heatmap a minute or two later, publishing the world too if it is new, and anyone can download it and sail against it with `Voyage` > `Compare a voyage`.
- **Entering a race:** Choose `Voyage` > `Enter a race` in Chartroom to check your run locally and open the prefilled form, or pick a race on the [Submit page](https://skeptic043.github.io/sailwind-chartroom-voyages/#submit) or choose `Upload a run` on a race's board. Older recordings without race markers or world metadata can still enter races and are marked with a note on the board.
- **Creating a race:** Plan a route in [Sailwind Chartroom](https://github.com/Skeptic043/sailwind-chartroom/releases/latest) and choose `Voyage` > `Publish route as race`, or build one directly on the [Chartroom Voyages website](https://skeptic043.github.io/sailwind-chartroom-voyages/#create). For a short race with small marks, it is recommended to mention in the description that racers should set Voyage Recorder's `Interval` to 60 seconds or lower. A longer interval can sail through a small mark without marking that it was crossed.
- **Fixing a rejected submission:** If a submission is not accepted, edit your first post on GitHub rather than opening a new issue and the check will run again automatically.

## Fair play

Times are self-reported, and the races are for fun. The checks are meant to catch honest mistakes, such as forgetting to turn off a mod, rather than deliberate cheating. Every upload is tied to a GitHub account, and uploads that break the rules or spoil the fun are removed.
